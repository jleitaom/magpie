# Roadmap

What comes after 0.1, in the order it's planned.

## v1: the Mac app

- **Organize.** Nest, rename, reorder and delete collections. Tags. Choose how a collection
  is sorted (newest, oldest, quickest, title, site, color, shuffle or your own order).
- **Sort Inbox.** Go through new Blocks one at a time from the keyboard: move, done, skip or
  delete. Reaching Inbox zero keeps a daily streak.
- **Journal.** A dense numbered list, next to the Gallery.
- **Smarter Blocks.** Recognize videos (with their length), products (with their price) and places.
- **Lucky Dip.** Pick one random Block from a pile (Read, Watch, Listen, Cook, Visit), limited
  to the time you have. Two re-spins a day, then you commit.
- **Rediscover.** A few random old saves to keep or archive. Collections whose Blocks expire,
  like a Watch later that empties itself after 30 days.
- **Speed.** Search everything, including text inside images. A global shortcut that saves the
  front Safari tab or the clipboard. Full keyboard control.
- **System.** Save from the Share menu, drop onto the Dock icon, turn a screenshot into a
  Block, and import bookmarks exported from Safari, Chrome or Firefox.

## v1.1: iPhone

- An iPhone app with a share sheet
- iCloud sync between the Mac and the iPhone. The data model already follows CloudKit's
  rules, so this needs no migration.

## v2

- Block types with their own fields, starting with a **Record** type for vinyl (Discogs
  lookup, barcode scanning, and a wishlist that turns into "owned")
- A free canvas Board view, stacks and a compare view
- Share a collection as a web page
- Find broken links
